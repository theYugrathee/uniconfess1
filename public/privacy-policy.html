Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

UniBee – Your Privacy Matters

UniBee (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the UniBee mobile application.

1 Information We Collect

a) Account Information

User ID, Username, Email address (if provided), College ID, Profile details (bio, avatar)

b) User Content

Confessions posted by users, Comments and likes, Messages sent within the app. Anonymous confessions do not publicly display your identity.

c) Device & App Information

Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token (for notifications), App usage data (basic analytics)

2 How We Use Your Information

We use your data to: Provide core app functionality, Show confessions relevant to your college, Send notifications for new confessions, likes, or messages, Maintain app security and prevent abuse. We do not sell your personal data.

3 Notifications

UniBee uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to send notifications. You can disable notifications anytime from your device settings.

4 Data Storage & Security

  • User data is securely stored on Supabase servers.
  • Notifications are handled via Firebase.
  • Sensitive credentials and admin keys are never exposed to users.
  • We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no system is 100% secure.

5 Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties, except: When required by law, To comply with legal obligations.

6 Children’s Privacy

UniBee is intended for users 16 years or older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

7 Your Rights

You have the right to: Access your data, Update or delete your account, Request data removal. You can contact us for any privacy-related requests.

8 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

9 Child Safety & CSAE Policy

Zero Tolerance Policy

Uniconfess strictly prohibits any form of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). We are committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users.

  • Sexual content involving minors is strictly forbidden.
  • Any form of exploitation, abuse, or grooming will result in an immediate permanent ban.
  • Sharing, requesting, or promoting inappropriate content related to minors is prohibited.

Any content found violating these rules will be immediately removed. Users involved may be permanently banned and reported to relevant authorities (NCMEC/Cyber Tipline) when required.
